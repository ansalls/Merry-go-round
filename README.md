# Merry-go-round

A curated collection of ideas, resources, and perspectives worth revisiting. The content here focuses on topics that felt significant enough on first encounter to warrant deeper thought — things that may shape where technology, work, and life are headed.

The primary lens is **AI and technology**: how to build in the space, how to innovate, and what approaches are likely to age well. A secondary lens is **productivity**: evidence-based strategies for doing more of what matters.

## Areas

- [AI](./ai/) — Building with AI, emerging architectures, tools, and ideas about where the field is going
- [Productivity](./productivity/) — Evidence-based approaches to getting the most out of your time and energy
